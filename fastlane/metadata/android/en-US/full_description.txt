SmartCraft for OsmAnd reads live engine data from a Mercury Marine SmartCraft gateway (the VesselView Mobile module) over Bluetooth LE and shows it as widgets on the OsmAnd or OsmAnd+ map, so you see your engine gauges and your chart on one screen.

<b>Widgets</b>
<ul>
<li>Engine RPM</li>
<li>Coolant, oil and seawater temperature</li>
<li>Battery voltage</li>
<li>Fuel flow and fuel tank level</li>
<li>Oil pressure and block pressure</li>
<li>Engine hours, fuel used and gear</li>
<li>Fuel efficiency, calculated from engine fuel flow and the phone's GPS speed (km/L, L/100km, NM/L, L/NM or US mpg)</li>
</ul>

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>Automatic reconnection to the gateway</li>
<li>Configurable units: °C/°F, kPa/bar/PSI, L/h or gal/h</li>
<li>Works with OsmAnd and OsmAnd+ (auto-detected)</li>
<li>Simulator mode to try the widgets without a boat</li>
<li>Debug log panel and optional log-to-file for diagnostics</li>
</ul>

<b>Requirements</b>
<ul>
<li>OsmAnd or OsmAnd+ installed. After starting the app, enable the SmartCraft widgets in OsmAnd: Menu → Configure screen.</li>
<li>A Mercury SmartCraft engine with a VesselView Mobile gateway</li>
<li>Bluetooth LE and location permission (location is needed for Bluetooth scanning and GPS speed)</li>
</ul>

No internet access, no ads, no tracking. The Bluetooth protocol was reverse engineered; some values (gear, fuel used, fuel flow calibration) are still being validated.
